WorkProof uses these standard concept URIs and adapts their definitions into
short Chinese summaries. These summaries were translated/paraphrased, include
an additional warning that metadata can be modified, and do not represent IPTC
endorsement, signature validation or verification of a file's actual origin.

Sources:
https://cv.iptc.org/newscodes/digitalsourcetype/trainedAlgorithmicMedia
https://cv.iptc.org/newscodes/digitalsourcetype/compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia
https://cv.iptc.org/newscodes/digitalsourcetype/algorithmicallyEnhanced

Only the first two concepts are treated as explicit generative-AI declarations.
Algorithmically enhanced content (such as sharpening or noise reduction) is
not by itself treated as a generative-AI declaration. No concept URI is fetched
when inspecting a file. Metadata is editable, and missing metadata does not
establish that no generative AI was used.
